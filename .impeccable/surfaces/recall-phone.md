# Recall phone, caregiver map and setup

Mode: Operate. The user explicitly requested a phone-first call, a caregiver graph, onboarding for photos/contacts/calendar, and renamed the app Recall. They chose Braille Institute typography, then rejected the bright palette and asked for substantially fewer distractions and choices. That latest instruction overrides the initial palette and AGENTS.md visual tokens.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A familiar photograph, one spoken thought, and an easy way to stop. Keep the person oriented without surrounding the conversation with a dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next throughout, self-hosted from the user's reference. Warm neutral #FAFAF7, ink #242C28, secondary #535E57, quiet #F0F2EC and #BDC5BD. No bright accents, shadows, serif display face, simulated waveform or decorative status dots.

STORY: Susan answers a familiar assistant, sees the current words, then separately chooses whether to remember and share. Family sees consented words and neutral per-topic counts. A caregiver can select photos, contacts and calendar events, review consent, and explore a simple memory map.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone header names Recall and Maya. The familiar photograph is the largest element during conversation, with short literal caption portions below it; no text overlays the image. The same photo stays still across turns. At the remember/share decision the photo gives way to the exact words and the question. Answer is the only filled primary action. Yes and No are equally prominent when a decision is needed; End call remains available below. Patient settings are moved to caregiver UI. The current sentence arrives in reserved word positions; reduced motion displays the full line at once.

FORM: User-specified simplification of a reference, built directly in code. No unapproved image comp. User-directed refinement of a supplied reference is the explicit exception to randomized concept selection. Native 390px and 320px iframe viewports test the phone; the caregiver has a wider map/detail composition which stacks on mobile. No claims of clinical improvement. The prototype is explicitly scripted and local.

PHOTO: User requested a photo-led flow on September 19. The beach image is explicitly AI-generated, fictional preview media only. It never enters the provenance graph or judged fixtures.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
