# Recall live patient call

Mode: Operate. The connected application supersedes the former scripted phone preview. No default family or generated familiar photograph is mounted.

THESIS: One current spoken prompt and an easy way to stop; keep conversation separate from caregiver management.

OWN-WORLD: Self-hosted Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, paper and ink, sage grouping and the same static five-node clay connected-path logo used on every route. Large neutral actions, visible focus and shared reading preferences.

STORY: A patient signs in with their issued key at `/sign-in`, continues to `/revisit`, and waits for an agreed scheduled browser call. Answer requests microphone access. The backend supplies Recall prompts, original-audio playback and listening steps. Store/share confirmation remains spoken and separately gated. End call closes the microphone and stops the flow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared identity header, patient access gate if needed, then call status. The live view identifies Recall as an AI assistant, names the topic when available and displays the current prompt or listening/playback state. No graph, statistics, sample transcript or prototype controls appear.

FORM: A 560px maximum phone column with large current text and neutral Answer/End actions. The earlier photo-led, paginated-caption design remains a brand target; it is not the mounted live composition. No client-side Yes/No confirmation substitutes for recorded responses.

BOUNDARIES: Real browser audio requires provider configuration, HTTPS or localhost and verified permissions. `/` is the account welcome, not the patient call. `/design/*` redirects to it. `/present` remains an isolated engine harness.
