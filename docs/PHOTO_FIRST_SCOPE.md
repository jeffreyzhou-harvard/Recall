# Photo-first family collection

This collection was imported from `Downloads/hack-mit-26/Relay` at the user's explicit September 20 request. The request includes photo dates/GPS extraction, OpenAI photo organization and transcription, email/password sign-in, invitation links and opt-in family reminders through Linq, and photo storytelling. It supersedes the older AGENTS.md restrictions on these specific features for this separate collection. The private patient-call graph, confirmation gates and clinical-language restrictions remain unchanged.

The family circle is a household-scoped shared photo collection. Joining a circle does not grant access to the patient’s private call evidence. OpenAI suggestions never become a patient or family member’s first-person testimony. People are counted visually; names are only explicit family labels. Automatic reminders require a joined member’s opt-in and explicit participation in the moment. Names are not inferred from faces.

Sign-in uses email/password or a single-use expiring invitation. Passwords use scrypt; browser sessions remain signed, HttpOnly, same-origin, role-scoped and revocable. The existing advanced key endpoint remains for compatibility, outside the normal product flow.
